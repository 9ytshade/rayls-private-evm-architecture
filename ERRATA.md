# Errata & Technical Scope Clarifications: Week 6 Submission
## "What is a Private EVM and Why Banks Need One"

Following reviewer evaluation and in accordance with institutional peer-review standards, this document formally records four technical clarifications and errata regarding the Week 6 research submission:

---

### 1. Telemetry Sampling Methodology & Stride
- **Article Text:** The article referred to a "500-block sample from Rayls Public Chain (Chain ID 72957)".
- **Formal Clarification:** As implemented in `scripts/rayls_node_telemetry_audit.py` (lines 91–93), the data collection used a stride of 10 (`step = 10`), querying **51 checkpoints sampled every 10th block across a 500-block span**. It was not a block-by-block consecutive scan. Future audits (Weeks 7 & 8) use true consecutive block-by-block scans (N = 1,000, step = 1) verifying all consecutive parent-hash links.

---

### 2. Simulated Model Outputs vs. Physical Node Benchmarks
- **Article Text:** Sections 4 and 5 discussed transaction processing latencies comparing Geth, Reth, and Rayls Sovereign.
- **Formal Clarification:** The figures generated by `scripts/private_evm_engine_benchmark.py` represent **theoretical mathematical model simulations** under parameterized queue and mempool assumptions. They do not constitute physical, measured benchmarks of running Geth, Reth, or Rayls production binaries. All future performance benchmarks (e.g. Week 8 ML-KEM-768 microsecond timings) execute compiled code on physical CPU hardware.

---

### 3. Reference Contract Gas Scope
- **Article Text:** Gas measurements were reported from `contracts/PrivateEVMInstitutionalGate.sol`.
- **Formal Clarification:** `PrivateEVMInstitutionalGate.sol` is an **illustrative reference prototype** designed to demonstrate institutional permissioning and sequential nonce execution in an isolated EVM environment. Gas figures measured across the 7 passing Foundry tests apply solely to this reference implementation and do not establish the gas consumption, throughput, or internal queuing behavior of Rayls' proprietary bank node software.

---

### 4. Network Domain Separation (Public L1 vs. Sovereign Subnet)
- **Article Text:** Public RPC observations were discussed alongside Sovereign private architecture.
- **Formal Clarification:** Telemetry gathered from `https://mainnet-rpc.rayls.com` (Chain ID 72957) reflects public network state only (block cadence, gasPrice, consensus contracts). These public observations do not establish or measure the gossip latency, transaction privacy, or consensus mechanisms of private, permissioned Rayls Sovereign subnets.
